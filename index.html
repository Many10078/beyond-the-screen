More Than a Game Worlds and Stories Create Your Story

Video games are usually seen as a way to have fun, but I think there is a lot more to them. Games combine art, music, storytelling, and interaction to create experiences that people can connect with.

What makes games different from other forms of art is that you are not just watching. You get to explore the world yourself and become part of the experience.

Through this website, I want to explore how games use creativity in different ways, from designing beautiful environments to telling stories and letting players create their own worlds.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can video games be considered art?

Yes. Video games combine visual design, music, storytelling, and interaction to create unique experiences.

What makes video games different from movies?

Games allow the audience to interact with the experience. Players can explore environments, make decisions, and sometimes shape the story themselves.

Does a game need realistic graphics to be art?

Not at all. A game's art style can be realistic, abstract, simple, or stylized.
